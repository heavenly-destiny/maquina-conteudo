Vision stated 19/11/2026 (community name not decided yet):
- A community "system" for top-of-funnel digital marketing people. Inside: several AI tools tanpz builds, his recorded lessons (including how to use each tool in their business), live tutoring sessions with invited guests.
- Tools planned: Forja (content machine), a sales-page cloner, an offer miner that finds scaled offers and organizes them on a canvas/board, a swipe file.
- Business model: each tool gets its own offer and sales page, sold directly via paid traffic. Every buyer lands inside the community system, where the tools they did not buy show as LOCKED, so they see them and ascend (buy more).
- He believes the best niche today is solving problems for people in digital marketing.

**Why:** shapes how every tool is designed: they must share one visual system and shell so they feel like one premium product, and each must be sellable alone.
**How to apply:** build Forja (and future tools) with a design system that can live inside a shared hub (same tokens, sidebar, account), and keep each tool's UI "muito foda" since it is also the product shown in ads. Related: [[maquina-v2-produto]], [[maquina-repositorio]].
