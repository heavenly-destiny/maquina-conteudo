A viral or high-CTR creative is dynamic and holds the viewer in **every second**, not just in the opening. The hook earns 3 seconds. The rest of the video has to keep re-earning attention by creating curiosity, so the viewer actually watches to the end.

**Why:** on 2026-11-16 the user said the "+3,4 mil seguidores em 7 dias" frame was a great hook and asked to test it as the opening. Then they stressed that "o resto do vídeo também precisa prender… a pessoa precisa ter curiosidade". A strong hook followed by a flat middle loses the view.

**How to apply:**
- Open a loop early ("vou te mostrar como", a number with no explanation yet, a blurred result) and close it late, at or near the CTA.
- Re-hook every ~3–5s with a new question, reveal, number jump or pattern interrupt (cut, zoom, sound hit, format switch). Avoid stretches longer than ~5s where nothing new is promised or revealed.
- Each beat should either answer the previous question or raise a new one. Ask "why would she keep watching at this second?" for every beat in the shot list.
- Hold back the payoff: tease the result first, then show the how, then reveal the full result.
- Hook-swap tests: put the follower-result hook in front of the approved creatives, but the body still has to carry its own curiosity.
