Every voice-over line must be illustrated on screen with the concrete scene it describes. The viewer should understand the line with the sound off. Grey tiles, empty cards, loaders and shapes are never enough.

**Why:** on 2026-11-16 the user watched the "perdia meia hora no Pinterest e nem ficava bom" beat. It showed only grey skeleton squares, and they said: "não entendi, só mostrou quadrado branco… você precisa mostrar isso de uma forma visual. SEMPRE".

**How to apply:**
- Before building, write a shot list in which every VO line gets its literal visual. "Pinterest for half an hour" means a real Pinterest-like feed full of real cheesy results, scrolling endlessly, with a clock running. "Nem ficava bom" means the chosen sticker pasted on the story with a white box, pixelated and crooked, plus a 🥴.
- Use real material (real app screens, real Pinterest results, real stickers), never placeholder shapes.
- During review, check each beat with the sound off and ask "does the picture say the line?". If it does not, rebuild that beat.
