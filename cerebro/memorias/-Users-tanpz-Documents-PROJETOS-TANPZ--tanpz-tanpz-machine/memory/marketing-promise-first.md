The user's core marketing belief is "se não prometer, não vende": without a promise, nothing sells. Every piece of copy for any of their offers (ads, creatives, sales pages, VSLs, scripts, emails, DMs) must carry a strong, explicit outcome promise up front, and repeat it at the call to action. Never write copy that only describes features or the tool.

**Why:** the user said so explicitly on 2026-11-16 ("no marketing digital ou em tudo se não prometer não vende, já pega essa visão") after early ad drafts were too soft. They expect this by default and do not want to ask for it each time.

**How to apply:**
- Open with the transformation or result the buyer wants: status, attention, admiration, time saved, money, relief. Say it with intensity and certainty ("vai chamar atenção de todo mundo", "em menos de 60 segundos", "você vira referência").
- Make every section ladder back to that promise: pain, then the promise, then the mechanism, then proof, then the promise again in the CTA.
- Keep the promise deliverable. Strong intensity is fine; a fabricated specific number is not ("+1.000 seguidores em 30 dias", guaranteed income). Meta rejects those ads and Brazilian consumer law (CDC) treats them as misleading. Mention this once if a draft crosses that line, then follow the user's call. Promises with numbers the product really delivers ("24 mil figurinhas", "em menos de 60 segundos") are encouraged.
