Stories Lab is a Brazilian web app (PWA, not in the app stores) for Instagram Stories. Brand: @storieslab.app. Support: https://storieslab.app/suporte. Payments go through Hubla, with PerfectPay for older sales.

**Promise (whole app, not only stickers):** more than 24 thousand stickers in more than 60 themes, with search (tap Copy, paste into the Story). It also has a photo editor with ready-made looks in the style of Lightroom presets, a camera with filters (photo and video), animated stickers, scripts and post ideas. Photograph, edit and decorate in one app, with no Canva, Drive or Lightroom. Gains: stop hunting for stickers, stop reusing Instagram's default stickers, and Stories look planned even when made in a rush.

**Plans (one-time payment, no auto-renewal):**
- Básico R$ 14,99: the stickers, 30 days of access. Checkout ZjRp7qhKO17c5BwcqW2n.
- Premium R$ 27,00: Básico plus Animadas and bonuses (shadows/light points, frames, backgrounds, Story videos, scripts, color palette), 1 year of access. New offers are QMj5JhJdg9dEnbG262ZQ and sXAlfbopdB6LNsF9u5AS. The special price is R$ 19,90, only after a price refusal.
- Vitalício R$ 14,90: add-on at checkout. Forever access to the app, updates and new content, keeping the purchased level.
- Extras: Personalizadas R$ 19,90 (50+ branded stickers within 24h), Kit Influencer Pro R$ 12,90 (Presets Pro, Agenda, Animadas, Magnéticos), Presets Pro R$ 9,90 (109 looks, 12 collections, unlimited editor), Agenda R$ 9,90 (30 days of ideas).
- Durations count from first access. Premium bought before 2026-11-15 never expires.

**Reason for the low price (owner, 2026-11-16):** it is the app's launch price ("preço de lançamento do app"), and it goes up after the launch. Use it as the price justification.

**New content:** the owner confirmed on 2026-11-16 that new content comes out every week, so "novidades toda semana" is true and may be used. The older review said "todo mês".

**Honesty limits (the owner's rules):**
- Social proof: say only "mais de 20 mil usuárias". The payments panel shows 21.135 sales. Do not invent other numbers.
- Testimonials: only the 5 real prints already on the site (prova_1 to prova_5). There is no founder bio, so invent none.
- Do not promise followers, sales or reach.
- The editor without "+100 filtros sem limite" belongs only to Presets Pro. Without it, saving is limited per day, so do not say "ilimitado" for Premium.
- Guarantee wording: "7 dias pra testar; não ficou satisfeita, devolvemos 100% do valor; o risco é nosso".
- The special condition lasts 24h, once per customer. Create no other fake urgency.
- The sticker limit is 100 different stickers per day; mention it only if asked.

**Audience:** women who post Stories: sellers on Instagram (shops, beauty, confectionery, fashion, services), creators, and people who post their routine. No design skill needed. Objections: "my Stories look plain", "I waste time searching", "I can't use Canva", "will it work on my phone", "is it a scam", "will I actually use it".
