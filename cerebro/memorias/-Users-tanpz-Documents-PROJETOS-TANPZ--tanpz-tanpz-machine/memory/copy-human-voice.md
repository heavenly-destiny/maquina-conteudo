All copy, narration and on-screen text must sound **as human as possible**: like a real person talking to a friend (for Stories Lab, a Brazilian woman who posts Stories), never like a corporate ad or AI-written text.

**Why:** the user asked for it explicitly on 2026-11-16 ("tem que ser o mais humano possível"), alongside the promise-first rule. Human-sounding creatives also look native in the feed, which drives CTR up and CPC down.

**How to apply:**
- Write spoken Brazilian Portuguese: "tô", "pra", "cê", "gente", "sério", "tipo", "olha isso", "juro". Use short broken sentences, with a hesitation or self-correction now and then ("eu achava que… sei lá, que era falta de jeito").
- Tell it as a personal story in the first person, with concrete everyday details (the coffee, the store, the Pinterest tab, 11pm), real feelings (a bit of shame, a "fiquei boba") and humor.
- Cut the AI and ad patterns: no "revolucione", "descubra o poder", "transforme sua vida", no rule-of-three lists, no polished slogans in the middle of the speech, no em-dash-heavy rhythm. The `humanizer` skill covers this.
- On-screen text looks like something typed in a real Story: lowercase, emojis, "kkkk", "gente 😭".
- The promise stays strong ([[marketing-promise-first]]), but it is said the way a friend would say it ("sério, todo mundo vai notar teu story").
- For voice-over, pick a natural conversational voice (not a radio announcer) and use emotion tags ([[voice-emotion-arc]]).

**Connection with the viewer (2026-11-16, "tem que ter conexão com quem está vendo"):** the viewer must feel "this is about me" within the first seconds.
- **Mirror her inner voice:** say out loud the thing she thinks and never says ("posto e fico olhando quem viu… e ninguém responde", "apago o story depois de 5 minutos de vergonha").
- **Specific moments she lives:** posting at night, checking the viewers list, seeing a friend's perfect Story, the product that won't sell, the empty DM. Concrete details create identification; generic pain does not.
- **Validate before selling:** "não é falta de talento, é falta da ferramenta certa". She is not the problem.
- **Speak to her, not to an audience:** "cê", "tu", "sabe quando…?", questions she answers in her head. Use the same words she uses.
- **Show her the future self:** the moment she gets the "que lindo!!" and feels proud. Connection is emotional first and logical second.
