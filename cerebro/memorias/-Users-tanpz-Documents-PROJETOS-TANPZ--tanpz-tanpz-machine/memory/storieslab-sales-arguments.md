On 2026-11-16 the owner said the copy must sell the outcome, not the tool. "Sem Canva, sem Drive" is weak on its own. Always work these angles, across many variations ("multiversos"):

- **Desires:** more engagement, more replies in the DMs, more followers, being noticed ("todo mundo vai notar você"), a profile people want to follow, Stories nobody skips.
- **Pain / fear of being left behind:** everyone she follows already posts polished Stories and is growing. She is the only one still posting plain Stories, stagnant while everyone else's engagement grows.
- **Objections to break:** "I can't design", "no time", "will it work on my phone", "is it a scam / too cheap", "will I actually use it", "monthly fee?". The answers are tap-copy-paste, 60 seconds, iPhone and Android in the browser, Hubla plus a 7-day guarantee, stickers for every niche and moment of the day, and a one-time payment.
- **Promise framing:** a Story that catches the eye in under 60 seconds, even without design skills, and a profile that stands out.

**Big idea (owner-refined, 2026-11-16):** she becomes the one everyone asks: "como você faz esses stories?", "como seus stories estão sempre tão lindos?", "como você tem tanto engajamento?". Speak about the whole result (beautiful stories, engagement, being admired). Do not narrow it to the sticker ("onde achou essa figurinha?"), which is only one example. It sells status, recognition and curiosity. Use it in the hero, the final call, the notifications and the ads.

**The owner's formula ("o segredo"):** the whole page runs as a cycle: hit the pain, show the desire, break an objection, repeat. Every button is a first-person NLP affirmation ("Eu quero me destacar", "Quero meus stories assim"). Decline or exit options are written as the painful choice, so leaving costs a thought, e.g. "Não, vou continuar com stories sem engajamento" instead of a neutral "Não quero". The owner took this from the Camys page (camyspacheco.com.br).

**Why:** the owner thinks the current page is weak on promise and does not speak to what the buyer really wants (growth and recognition).

**How to apply:** lead with desire and pain, then mechanism, then proof. Do not write guaranteed numeric results such as "vai dobrar seus seguidores em um mês". They are false claims, the Stories Lab sales rules forbid them, Meta ads reject them and Brazilian consumer law (CDC) treats them as misleading advertising. Use intensity without a guarantee instead: "chama muito mais atenção", "mais respostas", "seu perfil se destaca", "pare de ficar para trás". If the owner insists on a numeric claim, flag the risk again once and follow the owner's decision. Related: [[storieslab-offer]], [[storieslab-sales-page-brief]].

**Ad creatives (2026-11-16):** the owner wants an explicit outcome **promise** in every ad creative ("pode colocar promessa"). State the result strongly, e.g. "seus stories vão chamar atenção de todo mundo", "stories que ninguém pula", "você recebe muito mais respostas", "vira a pessoa que todo mundo pergunta 'como você faz?'". Still never give a numeric guarantee (followers or reach in X days). The ad copies live in storieslab/docs/criativos/.

**Creative assets rule (2026-11-16):** do not reuse any photos from the sales site in ad creatives: not the women in the app videos (beach, gym), not the extras art (Presets/Animadas/Agenda/Magnéticos), not the before/after pairs. Use fresh material instead: new screen recordings of the app from the owner, plus organic iPhone-style photos taken from Pinterest (the user approved this; see [[photo-style-aspirational]]) or supplied by the owner.
