# Estilo: Conteúdo orgânico (IA, tráfego, marketing)
References (all checked by watching): Df2yze7NUmc (VTurb podcast), Dfs0iS_jYnL (Mateus, IA para negócios), DcW33C-zyaN (Barsol), DeZck76xIf5 (Murilo Bono).

## Look
- Palette: dark wine/black base (#0e0608 to #2a0a10), ONE vivid accent (red #e11d2a or yellow #f5b000 for numbers), white text. One accent per video.
- Type: thin sans for the support words plus the keyword in heavy bold sans 2 to 3x larger, OR an italic serif in the accent color. Two families at most.

## Captions
- 2 to 4 words per group, built word by word on the speech timing (DTW). Each word: blur 12 to 0, y 16 to 0, 220 ms, power3.out.
- Keyword: scale contrast (2.5 to 3x) plus accent color. A number keyword "types" or counts up (8 DÍGITOS, 11.764).
- Position: free around the face (side or chest height), never covering the mouth. Optional: the giant keyword sits BEHIND the speaker (needs a person cutout, see Barsol).

## Motion and B-roll
- Every 3 to 5 s, the screen switches to a full motion scene on wine: glass cards (backdrop blur, 1px border at rgba(255,255,255,.12), red glow) showing a recreated UI of the tool being talked about (list, counter, progress bar, typed field, cursor clicking).
- Proof shows up as a card synced to the word (number, print, profit).
- Slight parallax on the cards (y ±8 px, sine) plus a slow push (scale 1 to 1.04).

## Rhythm and transitions
- Hard cut from the talking head to the motion scene. Between motion scenes: crossfade plus blur 250 ms. Jump cut with a 1.0 to 1.08 push on the talking head every 2 to 4 s.
- No intro and no logo at the start: the first second already carries the number or the claim.
- Ending: "Comente X" pill in the accent color (VTurb: "Comente 'Episódio 182'").

## Sound
- Low music bed under the voice. Short whoosh on each motion scene entry, tick when a card lands.
