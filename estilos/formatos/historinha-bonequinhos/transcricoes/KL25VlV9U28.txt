He accidentally swallowed a produce sticker when he was eating a pear and began to cough, causing the sticker to travel back up his throat into his nasal cavity where it got stuck without him realizing it was there. For seven years, he had sinus inflammation, bad breath, and difficulty breathing, so he used an over-the-counter inhaler thinking he had asthma. Until one day, he finally went to a doctor who was surprised to discover the
sticker and removed it.
