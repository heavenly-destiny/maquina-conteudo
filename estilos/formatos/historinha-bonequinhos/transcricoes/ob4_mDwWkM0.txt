If a hummingbird's heart is removed, then stuck into your chest, and it was beating almost 20 times a second, could it rapidly pump blood around your body, giving you superhuman endurance? Well, within moments, your brain would start shutting down because the tiny heart just couldn't pump enough blood through your body. For this to actually work, you would need thousands of hummingbird hearts beating together just for your muscles to get extra oxygen, meaning you could run for much longer and recover much faster
but this would probably destroy your blood vessels and you'd have to spend most of the day eating just to keep all those hearts beating.
