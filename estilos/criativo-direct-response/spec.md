# Estilo: Criativo de venda (direct response)
References: DfYixEaAQfs (Guilherme Serraglio, Criativos que Escalam) as the main one, plus the scaled ads in the Radar (UTMify, CASSIM, Pedro Sobral).

## Structure
- 0 to 3 s: the proof is ALREADY on screen. Example: two ad cards pinned at the top (ROI 0,99 vs 3,03) while the speaker delivers the hook.
- Body: each claim gets a proof card that pops in on the matching word (profit R$ 124.675,21, a print, a verified-badge phone mockup, a document).
- Close: direct CTA, ideally "Comente X" or "Saiba mais".

## Captions
- One word at a time, chest height, white with a soft shadow, swapped with the speech. Minimal animation (pop 0.9 to 1, 120 ms) so the attention stays on the proof.

## Motion
- Proof cards: pop-in with back.out(1.6), 280 ms, long soft shadow, 16 to 20 px radius. They hold for at least 1.2 s so the number gets read.
- Jump cuts with reframing (1.0 / 1.1 push) every 2 to 3 s.

## Rule
- The copy sells; the edit only proves it. No heavy motion competing with the number.
