# Bulk Scheduler

You can now bulk schedule your clips directly from the results page.

1. **Navigate to your project**: Open your projects' results page.
2. **Select Clips**:
   * Click on "**Select**" in the top right corner of the results page.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/bulk-scheduler-1.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=71c705c6298c7a379a35d51b81197732)

OR

* Click on the **calendar icon** for a given clip and then click on "**Bulk Schedule**"

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/bulk-scheduler-2.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=58aab94b68b724a635a68137516ae88c)

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/bulk-scheduler-3.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=1a96d5b542a7f40ffdaefdd5d2e701fc)

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/bulk-scheduler-4.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=97a39b065e8edee4ffe094bb61a9fa7c)

3. Check the box next to each clip to make your selection or use "**Select all**" to choose all clips at once

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/bulk-scheduler-5.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=8f5e0f3dd3269240cb025321676d51fb)

4. After choosing your clip, click on "**Bulk Schedule**" in the top right corner

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/bulk-scheduler-6.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=7e67490f832615b97a43db56ad9cdc64)

5. **Configure your scheduling**: After selecting your clips, you'll now set when and how they will be published in bulk. This step defines the **posting order, start time, and frequency** — our AI will handle the rest.
   * **Arrange the clip order**: Manually assign the order: Click on the number next to each clip and enter its position in the posting sequence (e.g., 1 for the first clip, 2 for the second).

> [!NOTE]
>
> This number determines the exact order in which your clips will be published. For example: A clip marked "1" will go out first, a clip marked "2" will be posted next, and so on.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/bulk-scheduler-7.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=cdede66cc1ea93d85ce12857b6d22fd3)

* **Set time & frequency**:
* Pick the date and time you want the first clip (order 1) to be published.
* Click of "**Frequency**" to define how often you want each clip to be published after the first one.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/bulk-scheduler-8.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=2986241fbfc281127ca6deb953d9da41)

6. Our AI will automatically create:

* **Engaging descriptions**
* **Relevant hashtags**

7. **Check schedule**: You can **preview times and edit captions**. Once you're happy with the order, start time, and frequency, click the “**Schedule**” button.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/bulk-scheduler-9.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=2a74f85a09306f5029b5155fe8cc2413)

8. All your selected clips will now be **scheduled for automatic posting** in the order and timing you configured. To **review or make changes**:

* Head to the **Calendar page** (accessible via the **left-hand thumbnail sidebar**).

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/bulk-scheduler-10.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=252e8e6c9b27e08f187fb283b454d983)

* There, you can **view all scheduled posts, adjust posting times, reschedule or delete** any clip individually.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/bulk-scheduler-11.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=bdcbea18afc5e6e877f44ee6f5e8d09d)

# FAQ

## Who can use the Bulk Scheduling feature?

This feature is **available exclusively to Pro users**. If you're on a Free, Trial or Starter plan you'll need to upgrade to access bulk scheduling.

## Which platforms are supported?

Currently, **Bulk Scheduling is only available for YouTube**. Support for additional platforms is coming soon!

## Does bulk scheduling to X use credits?

Yes. Each clip you schedule to X uses 3 credits per X account. For example, scheduling 10 clips to one X account uses 30 credits. You'll see the total at the bottom of the scheduling window before you confirm.

## How does the posting frequency work?

You set a **fixed interval** (e.g., 2 hours , 4 hours, or daily), and clips will be scheduled **one after the other** based on the order you defined.

* The system follows your selected time interval **exactly**.
* **It does not skip weekends, holidays, or “ideal” posting times**.
* Example: If you schedule 5 clips and your first post starts at **Monday 9:00 AM** with a **2-hour interval**, the next clips will post at **10:00 AM (clip 2)**, **12:00 PM (clip 3)**, **2:00 PM (clip 4)**, **4:00 PM (clip 5)** — even across weekends or nights.

## Can I change the schedule after I’ve set it?

Yes. After scheduling, go to the **Calendar page** (via the left-hand sidebar) to:

* View your scheduled posts
* **Edit posting times**
* Reschedule or delete individual clips

## Can I schedule the same clip to post on multiple days?

Yes. However, each clip can be scheduled **once per session**. If you’d like to repost the same clip, you need to schedule it again.

## Can I bulk schedule to multiple platforms at once?

Not yet. At this time, **bulk scheduling supports YouTube only**. Posting to additional platforms is coming soon.

## Can I pause or cancel a scheduled post?

Yes. Visit the **Calendar page** (via the left-hand sidebar), find the clip, and:

* **Edit the time**
* **Delete the scheduled post**
* (Coming soon: pause/resume features)

## How many clips can I schedule at once?

There's no hard limit for Pro users.

## Do scheduled clips auto-publish even if I close the app?

Yes! Once scheduled, your clips will continue to be posted automatically — even if you close the browser, log out, or if your subscription expires.
