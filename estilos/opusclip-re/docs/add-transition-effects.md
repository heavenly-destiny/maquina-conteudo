# Add Transition Effects

## Turn on Auto Transition

Turn on **Auto Transition** in the **Transitions** panel in the left sidebar of the editor. You can also turn it on from Quick Start, your Brand Template, or AI Enhance.

Auto Transition adds a 0.5-second Flash black transition at each cut where both clips are at least 1 second long and one of them is at least 5 seconds long. To use a different style, choose it from the list under the switch.

## Add a transition manually

Hover between two clips on the timeline and click the **Transition** icon. You can also drag a style onto a cut, or click a style in the **Transitions** panel to add it to the cut nearest the playhead.

Pick a style from the list: Slide left, Slide right, Slide up, Slide down, Flash black, Flash white, Blur, Push in, Light, Wink, Stretch, Flip, Film burn, or Glitch.

To use the same style on every cut, click **Apply to all**.

## Adjust or remove a transition

Click a transition on the timeline to select it. Drag its edges, or use the **Duration** slider, to change how long it lasts. The default is 0.5 seconds. You can set it from 0.2 to 5 seconds, or to half the length of the shorter clip if that is less.

To replace a transition, pick another style. To remove it, select **None**, or select the transition and press Delete.

Both clips at a cut must be at least 1 second long. If one is shorter, the editor shows: "Transitions require two adjacent clips, each at least 1 second long."

> [!NOTE]
>
> Transitions work in the web editor only. They are not available in the mobile apps, in XML export, or through the API and MCP.

## Looking for the previous effects?

The six previous effects (cross fade, cross zoom, zoom in, zoom out, fade in, and fade out) now work on a single clip. You can find them under **Transitions > Animation > Custom animation**.

## Using Auto Transition in a Brand Template?

If your Brand Template used Auto Transition with an older effect, it now shows **Needs Update**. Pick a new effect to keep using it on new videos. Projects you've already edited stay unchanged.
