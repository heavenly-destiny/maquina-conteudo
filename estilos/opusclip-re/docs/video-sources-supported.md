# Video Sources Supported

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P/images/imgs/screenshot0408-05.PNG?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P\&q=85\&s=9c52b5ee33a632bc4cc9c4ec400686f3)

Uploading your videos to be clipped by OpusClip is as easy as ever! We currently support links from:

* **Apple Podcast**
* **YouTube**
* **Google Drive**
* **Vimeo**
* **Zoom**
* **Zoom Clips**
* **Rumble**
* **Twitch**
* **Facebook**
* **LinkedIn**
* **X**
* **Dropbox**
* **Riverside**
* **Loom**
* **Frame.io**
* **StreamYard**
* **Kick**
* **[Medal.tv](https://medal.tv/)**
* **Public .mp4 urls**
* **Local video uploads(max size: 30GB for Pro Users)**

> [!TIP]
>
> Please make sure the links you submit are public or unlisted. Private videos can not be accessed.
