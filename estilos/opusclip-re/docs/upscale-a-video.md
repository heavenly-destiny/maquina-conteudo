# Upscale a Video

If your footage is blurry or low-resolution, Upscale uses AI to make it sharper, clearer, and more high-definition — no editing experience required.

## How to upscale a video

1. From the Quick Start menu, click **Upscale**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/V3qDnTGm-ICir8pe/images/imgs/upscale-video-quick-start.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=V3qDnTGm-ICir8pe\&q=85\&s=93f0176beb93d9ea3fac77fd7a28d3c5)

2. Add your video: paste a YouTube link, click **Upload** to select a file from your device, or click **Google Drive** to import.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/V3qDnTGm-ICir8pe/images/imgs/upscale-video-add.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=V3qDnTGm-ICir8pe\&q=85\&s=7f7da3c8655ccd840526cef510c04ea8)

3. Once your video is loaded, review the preview and choose **Upscale to HD** or **Upscale to 4K** to begin processing. Each button displays the credit cost for that option, based on your video's length and selected resolution. Credits are charged when processing starts — see [Credit cost](#credit-cost) below.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/V3qDnTGm-ICir8pe/images/imgs/upscale-video-hd-4k.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=V3qDnTGm-ICir8pe\&q=85\&s=9aab21d8b052ca73daea70872b377bfa)

4. When upscaling is complete, open the **History** tab and click **Download** to save your enhanced video. Upscaled videos are available for 7 days.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/V3qDnTGm-ICir8pe/images/imgs/upscale-video-history.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=V3qDnTGm-ICir8pe\&q=85\&s=4e96d8d5b656586ceb01a3c8d9b65c2f)

You can also access the upscaled video from **Recent projects** on the homepage.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/V3qDnTGm-ICir8pe/images/imgs/upscale-video-recent.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=V3qDnTGm-ICir8pe\&q=85\&s=8d751497b436d553a9527c860afcf1e5)

## Credit cost

On **Pro**, **Max**, and **Business**, upscaling costs credits based on the length of your video:

* **HD:** 11.7 credits per 10 seconds of video
* **4K:** 46.8 credits per 10 seconds of video

Video length is rounded up to the next 10 seconds.

### Example calculations

| Video length | HD (1080p) | 4K |
| - | - | - |
| 30 seconds | 35 credits (3 × 11.7) | 140 credits (3 × 46.8) |
| 45 seconds | 58 credits (5 × 11.7) | 232 credits (5 × 46.8) |

## FAQ

**How long does upscaling take?**

Processing time varies by video length. You can monitor progress in the History tab — it shows a live percentage as your video upscales.

**How long are my upscaled videos saved?**

Upscaled videos are available to download from the History tab for 7 days.

**What is the maximum video length?**

HD (1080p) upscaling supports videos up to 30 minutes, while 4K upscaling supports videos up to 5 minutes.

**What counts as blurry or low-res footage?**

Upscale is designed to improve footage that lacks sharpness or clarity — for example, old recordings, compressed video, or anything that looks grainy or pixelated.

**How is HD upscaling charged?**

On Pro, Max, and Business, HD (1080p) upscaling costs 11.7 credits per 10-second block (rounded up). The total credit cost is displayed on the **Upscale to HD** button before processing begins.

**How is 4K upscaling charged?**

4K upscaling costs 4 times as many credits as HD (1080p) upscaling. The total credit cost is displayed on the **Upscale to 4K** button before processing begins.
