# Add Intro/Outro Cards

Want to add a **call to action (CTA)** or **provide more context** for your clip? With the **Pro plan**, you can enhance your clips by uploading videos or images to use as intro or outro cards. Here's how to do it:

1. **Navigate to the Editor**: Open the specific clip editor page you want to work on.
2. **Upload Media**:
   * Go to the right panel and select "**Media**".
   * Click the "**+ Upload**" area to import the media you wish to use.
3. **Add Media to Intro/Outro**: You can apply the uploaded media in two ways:
   * Click "**Add as Intro**" or "**Add as Outro**"
   * Drag the specific media and drop it into the intro or outro placeholder at the start or end of the clip on the timeline.
