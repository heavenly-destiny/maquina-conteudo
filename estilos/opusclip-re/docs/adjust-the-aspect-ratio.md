# Change Aspect Ratio

You can change your aspect ratio in **Editor > Clip Layout Settings**.

You can adjust your aspect ratio to either **9:16 (Vertical), 1:1 (Square), 16:9 (Landscape) or 4:5 (Portrait)**. Once you've chosen your desired ratio, please click **Save Changes** to apply.

If you'd like to change the aspect ratio of your Crop, please check out [this article](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/can-i-customize-crop-ratio).
