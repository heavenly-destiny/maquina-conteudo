# Add Logo and Custom Fonts

## Upload logo

To upload your logo or a overlay, you can either click **Upload** and upload your asset, or click **Brand kit** and select a kit which contains your logo or overlay.

## How do I add a custom font?

Pro plan users can add custom or brand fonts for captions.

### How to add custom fonts to a clip

1. Click **Captions** in the editor and go to **Font** tab, select **Custom fonts** from the dropdown list. You'll be taken to your Asset Library.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P/images/imgs/screenshot0408-08.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P\&q=85\&s=6cd6873df9373ecda702ffe98dee8d38)

2. Here, Pro plan users can upload up to 2 fonts. Currently OTF and TTF font formats are supported.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/VHEffbsgbIX8L5Jz/images/imgs/snapshot-79.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=VHEffbsgbIX8L5Jz\&q=85\&s=80d66f610c08443f9314509d919213de)

3. Once uploaded your font will be available in the editor.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P/images/imgs/screenshot0408-09.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P\&q=85\&s=e03afc8f9ad33fc91918bd06ee1d51f8)

### How to add custom fonts to a Brand Kit

1. Navigate to your **Brand kit** page and click the add custom font button in your Brand Kit. You'll be taken to your **Asset Library**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/VHEffbsgbIX8L5Jz/images/imgs/snapshot-81.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=VHEffbsgbIX8L5Jz\&q=85\&s=0f5f11bbf4948b6e4f115c06181638f9)

2. Pro plan users can upload up to 2 fonts. Currently OTF and TTF font formats are supported.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/VHEffbsgbIX8L5Jz/images/imgs/snapshot-82.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=VHEffbsgbIX8L5Jz\&q=85\&s=33d602f19d469fb696cb2f37dcf76370)

3. Once uploaded your font will be available to add to your **Brand Kit**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/VHEffbsgbIX8L5Jz/images/imgs/snapshot-83.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=VHEffbsgbIX8L5Jz\&q=85\&s=b30b6d1055ab81a44e2d95c4d5eb445d)

4. Make sure to save your changes!

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/VHEffbsgbIX8L5Jz/images/imgs/snapshot-84.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=VHEffbsgbIX8L5Jz\&q=85\&s=1351e1d7ec8671d858b377a72a00e70f)

### How do I manage uploaded fonts?

You can manage your custom fonts in the **Asset Library**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/XStAq9UXRcMPMTAr/images/imgs/snapshot-85.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=XStAq9UXRcMPMTAr\&q=85\&s=e6aa65d8e99d836e033a81c42e1bb3e4)

### How many custom fonts can I upload?

Pro plan users can upload 2 custom fonts.
