# What is the Virality Score on OpusClip?

## What is the Virality Score on OpusClip?

For users on **Pro** and **Starter** plans, our advanced AI analyzes your clips and assigns Virality Scores based on how likely each clip is to go viral. You can find the Virality Score next to your clips, depending on the layout you use to view your clips.

The Virality score ranges from **0** to **99**, with higher scores suggesting a greater chance of engagement and shares on social media. Each clip will have its own Virality Score, allowing you to compare its performance relative to others. By default, your clips will be sorted by Virality Score, from highest to lowest on the results page.

## How the Virality Score is Calculated

OpusClip’s AI evaluates **multiple aspects** of your video to determine its Virality Score:

* **Hook**: Does the introduction grab attention and directly relate to the main topic of the video?
* **Flow**: Does the video flow logically from one part to the next, with a satisfying conclusion?
* **Value**: Does the video offer value, resonate emotionally, and create a personal connection with the audience?
* **Trend**: Is the video aligned with current trends and audience interests?
  In addition to these factors, our AI performs extra checks to ensure it finds the best clips for your **specific needs**. For example, it will also evaluate whether the clip is relevant to your prompt, when you are using the [ClipAnything](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/9947095-clip-anything.md) model.

## Why the Virality Score Matters

The Virality Score provides insight into your clips' potential to go viral. **Higher scores** indicate a greater likelihood of attracting attention, engagement, and shares. It will guide you in understanding your clips and optimizing your content strategy for better results.

## Who Can Access the Virality Score?

The Virality Score feature is available exclusively to **Pro** and **Starter** plan users. Free plan users will not be able to see the Virality Score for their clips.
