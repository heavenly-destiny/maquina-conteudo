# Customize My Posts

## Add hashtags for my social posts

### Add hashtags

You can add hashtags when scheduling your posts by directly typing the hashtags. The hashtags will be automatically recognized by different platforms.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/XStAq9UXRcMPMTAr/images/imgs/snapshot-86.png?s=c91ca8c2b13566c9dfc7548481c3599f)

### Popular hashtag trends

For TikTok, you can also search for popular hashtags and see their trendiness. You will see how many times the hashtag has been used.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/XStAq9UXRcMPMTAr/images/imgs/snapshot-87.png?s=ce56c3087b6a2fc062b5103d9fbd6690)

## Tag other collaborators in my post

You can now tag other collaborators when scheduling an Instagram post. You just need to type the correct handle in the post descriptions.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/XStAq9UXRcMPMTAr/images/imgs/snapshot-88.png?s=81442226c4ace27f1549a8103cc3c1d2)

### AI Features / Post Customization

AI-generated titles, descriptions, and hashtags are also available and editable before posting on the **OpusClip iOS app**.

### Limitations:

* The tags have to refer to a valid Instagram handle, and the account must be public.

* Now we only support tagging other users on Instagram. If you want this feature for other platforms, please let us know by clicking upvote for that feature!

## Include YouTube link to full video in my social posts

You can now add the full video link automatically when publishing posts to YouTube. Just tick the **"Add the full video link to your YouTube post"** checkbox — the link lands between your caption and hashtags.

![](https://public.cdn.opus.pro/changelog/include-link-to-full-video-0904.avif)

### Limitations:

* Available on OpusClip Web only.

* We currently only support adding the full video link when publishing to YouTube. If you want this feature for other platforms, please let us know by clicking upvote for that feature!
