# Fix lighting and color with Auto color correction

Auto color correction makes poorly lit footage look professionally graded. OpusClip analyzes your video and fixes white balance, brightness, and contrast, so you get publish-ready color without touching a single slider. It's free to use.

# Before you start

* **Plans:** Available on Trial, Pro, Max, and Business plans.
* **Cost:** Free for now.
* **Best results:** Talking-head videos with dim, uneven, or off-color lighting.

# Turn on Auto color correction

You can turn it on in three places: when you set up your Recipe workflow, in the editor sidebar, or above the preview in the editor.

## When you set up your workflow

1. Upload your video and choose **Auto Edit** or **"Get clips in 1 click"**.
2. In the workflow settings, turn on **Auto color correction**.
3. Start processing.

Every video or clip from this workflow gets color corrected.

## In the editor sidebar

1. Open your video or clip in the editor.
2. In the right sidebar, click **AI enhance**.
3. Turn on **Auto color correction**.

# Adjust the intensity

If the result looks too strong, dial it back for a more natural look.

1. In the editor, click **AI enhance**.
2. Under **Auto color correction**, drag the **Intensity** slider. The default is 100%.
3. Preview your video and adjust until you're happy with it.

# FAQ

**Does Auto color correction use credits?**

It doesn't use credits right now.

**What does Auto color correction change?**

It balances white balance, brightness, and contrast so skin tones look natural and the scene looks evenly lit. It doesn't apply a stylized filter.

**Why didn't my video improve much?**

Auto color correction works best on clear footage with fixable lighting. Results may be limited on blurry video or footage with extreme contrast, like a bright window behind a dark subject.

**Can I undo it?**

Yes. Turn off **Auto color correction** in **AI enhance** or above the preview at any time to restore your original colors.
