# Clip a Specific Timeframe

If you only need to process a specific portion of a 2-hour video, OpusClip makes it easy!

Use our **Co-Pilot** feature to select and clip a specific timeframe from your video. After submitting your video, simply choose the desired start and end times to focus on just the part you need.
