# Edit, Rename, Duplicate, or Delete a Recipe

Manage your saved Recipes from **Home → See all Recipes → Manage Recipes → My custom Recipes**.

## How do I edit a Recipe?

1. Find your custom Recipe and open its **•••** menu.
2. Select **Edit**.
3. Adjust the Recipe settings.
4. Save the changes to the current Recipe, or save them as a new Recipe to keep both versions.

## How do I rename a Recipe?

1. Open the **•••** menu on your custom Recipe.
2. Select **Rename**.
3. Enter the new name and confirm the change.

## How do I duplicate a Recipe?

Open the **•••** menu on your custom Recipe and select **Duplicate**.

Use the copy to make another version of your setup. For example, keep one Recipe for captioned clips and another that also adds B-roll.

## How do I delete a Recipe?

Open the **•••** menu on your custom Recipe and select **Delete**. Follow the on-screen prompt to finish.

## Can I change a built-in Recipe?

You can adjust its settings for a video and save your version as a custom Recipe. You can't overwrite or delete the built-in Recipe itself.
