# Add Social Accounts

> [!NOTE]
>
> If you are a **Pro plan user**, you can connect **multiple accounts** to each social platform.
>
> Now we support autoposting to **YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Page, LinkedIn Business, X, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky**

1. Click the **Social accounts** tab

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC/images/imgs/snapshot-116.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC\&q=85\&s=b2be4a08ce74a7a8a88e907a65e9af9b)

2. Connect your accounts to various platforms

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC/images/imgs/snapshot-117.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC\&q=85\&s=f7f718d0c7ab7db7cb9bc0ecb3f16d8c)

3. Choose the platform you'd like to connect to, then follow the provided instructions to link your account.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC/images/imgs/snapshot-118.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC\&q=85\&s=c859a2db860eaefd214bc12e26151a45)

> [!NOTE]
>
> **NOTE:** You can also connect and manage your social accounts from the **OpusClip mobile app**. Go to your Profile page → Social accounts to add or remove connected platforms.
