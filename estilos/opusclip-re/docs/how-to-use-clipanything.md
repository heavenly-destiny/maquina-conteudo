# How to use ClipAnything?

ClipAnything is the *first-ever multimodal AI clipping that lets you clip any moment from ANY video* using visual, audio, and sentiment cues, including videos with little to no dialogue.

You can use *natural language prompts to find any scene, action, character, event, emotional moment, viral topic, and more*.

When processing videos with minimal talking or less talking, we **highly recommend that you use ClipAnything** to improve the quality of your clips.

1. **Drop your video link / Upload a video**

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/clipanything-1.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=61e31e624b2925781dc7ea7d6c318e7e)

2. **Select "ClipAnything" under Clip model**

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/clipanything-2.gif?s=9860c4bcdb94ed6548d45eb89fe49c7b)

3. **Select your video genre** (optional)

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/clipanything-3.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=03181d4b4b5b9c326424b9b5dc860f83)

4. **Type your prompt to find a specific moment** (optional). For instance, "Find all the touchdowns", or "Find the moments most likely to go viral on social media."

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4/images/imgs/clipanything-4.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4oBcsSmINxEJFGA4\&q=85\&s=402dc38ba7da35ee9bf1a98df4a80435)

> [!TIP]
>
> You can save your favourite clip settings (model, genre, and presets) as default to simplify your workflow and never miss a setting again.

5. **Choose a caption style from Quick Presets**, or select **No Caption** if your video already has captions baked in or you prefer a clean look.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/GsH3gXLY0N7C1KSM/images/imgs/clipanything-no-caption.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=GsH3gXLY0N7C1KSM\&q=85\&s=adc2cac0deba6bc43bb6ff208f74077f)

Please let us know about your experience using OpusClip, by clicking the “heart” or “thumbs down” button located on the left side of each clip, or by completing our quick feedback survey when you see it on the results page.
