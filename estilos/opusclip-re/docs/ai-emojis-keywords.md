# AI Emojis/Keywords

OpusClip uses AI to automatically add relevant emojis to your subtitles.

## How to turn on AI emojis?

You can click into **"AI Enhance"** from right sidebar and toggle on **"AI Emoji"** feature.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P/images/imgs/screenshot0409-11.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P\&q=85\&s=909d971a374f90070dbaea32063053e2)

## How to edit emojis?

You can click a word and then select **Emoji** option from the dropdown list of **Add**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P/images/imgs/screenshot0409-01.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P\&q=85\&s=398445f56462ba55536019e29ff97089)
