# Add motion graphics to your video

Motion graphics are animated text and graphics that appear at key moments in your video. In the Auto Edit recipe, OpusClip reads your transcript, finds what's worth highlighting, like a guest's name, a big number, or an important date, and adds a matching graphic at the right moment. No more hunting for the right moment, finding an element, and typing in the text by hand.

You can also add motion graphics manually from the editor.

# Before you start

* **Plans:** Available on Trial, Pro, Max, and Business plans.
* **Credits:** Automatic motion graphics use credits based on video length. See **How credits work** below.
* **Best results:** Talking-head videos with clear speech and specific details, such as names, numbers, and dates.

# Turn on motion graphics in Auto Edit

1. Upload your video and choose the **Auto Edit** recipe.
2. In the recipe settings, turn on **Motion graphics**.
3. Start processing.

You can also turn on **Motion graphics** when you clip a long video.

If you also turned on an AI viral hook, your motion graphics match the style of your hook template, so the whole video looks consistent.

# Add a motion graphic in the editor

1. Open your video in the editor.
2. Move the playhead to where you want the graphic to appear.
3. In the right sidebar, click **Graphics**.
4. Browse by category, or search for a template.
5. Click a template to add it.

| Category | Use it to | Examples |
| - | - | - |
| Data | Turn numbers into visuals viewers can scan quickly | Big number, bar chart, trend line, gauge |
| Social | Add social proof and drive engagement | Follow button, like burst, review card |
| Promo | Create urgency and drive action | Save the date, countdown timer, sale badge |

Looking for animated text, like name tags and keyword pop-ups? You'll find them under **Text**.

# Edit a motion graphic

Select a graphic in the preview or on the timeline to open **Motion graphic settings**. You can:

* **Edit the content:** Change the text, number, or label.
* **Style it:** Change the font, text color, and background color, or apply bold, italic, or underline.
* **Resize and move it:** Drag the corners to resize, or drag the graphic to reposition it.
* **Change the timing:** Drag the edges of the graphic on the timeline. A shorter graphic animates faster; a longer one animates slower.
* **Hide captions:** Turn off captions while the graphic is on screen.

# Delete a motion graphic

1. Select the graphic on the timeline.
2. Press **Delete**, or click the delete icon.

# How credits work

Automatic motion graphics in Auto Edit use 6 credits per minute of uploaded video. In a clipping project, they use 6 credits per clip. From the editor, manually adding motion graphics is free of charge.

# FAQ

**Why didn't OpusClip add a graphic where I expected?**
OpusClip highlights the moments most likely to keep viewers watching and spaces graphics out to avoid clutter. You can add any graphic yourself from the Graphics panel.

**Does it work with multiple speakers or split-screen layouts?**
Motion graphics work best with a single speaker. With multiple people on screen or changing layouts, check the placement and adjust it in the editor.

**Do motion graphics add sound?**
No. Motion graphics are visual only.
