# Manual Reframe

To manually adjust your video's framing, you can open the crop window to manually reframe your content. There are 3 ways to open the crop window:

* Click the Crop button
* Double click the scene
* Press **X**

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P/images/imgs/manual-reframe-new-1.gif?s=b65c5f1d557bb40e1ab67a285dc20a6a)

You can also select multiple scenes and update their layouts all at once. Hold Shift to select multiple scenes, then right-click to update their layouts all at once.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P/images/imgs/manual-reframe-new-2.gif?s=1951f7752e8f15e20f49e3cfacfb81d8)
