# Add B-roll to your video

B-roll keeps viewers watching by showing what you're talking about. OpusClip finds the best moments for B-roll and fills them with AI images, AI video, or stock footage. You get to review every spot before anything is added.

## What types of B-roll can I add?

* **AI image:** images generated for each spot. All images are planned together from your whole video, so they stay on topic and share one consistent style.
* **AI video:** short video clips generated for each spot. By default, **AI Style** matches the color, lighting, and camera feel of your footage. You can also pick a preset style.
* **Stock video:** footage from our stock library that matches each spot.

You can mix B-roll types in the same clip.

## Add B-roll automatically

### From the clips page

You can trigger the B-roll functionality directly next to clips you want to add B-rolls to. This will take you to the editor and trigger the **Find spots** functionality in the editor below.

In Grid view, hover over the scissor icon to find **Add B-roll**.

In List view, hover over the **AI tools** button to find **Add B-roll**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/WMXFusCPuQBQuKUM/images/imgs/ai-broll-clips-list.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=WMXFusCPuQBQuKUM\&q=85\&s=d4a89a80644639def20acefcad5f87c6)

### From the editor

* Open a clip in the editor and click **B-roll** in the right-side panel.
* Click **Find spots**. OpusClip reads your transcript and suggests where B-roll fits. This takes about 30 seconds.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/WMXFusCPuQBQuKUM/images/imgs/ai-broll-find-spots.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=WMXFusCPuQBQuKUM\&q=85\&s=1e2f519cea7d41fa45d1619100b24498)

* Review the suggested spots and deselect any you don't want.
* Under **Fill with**, choose **AI image**, **AI video**, or **Stock video**.
* For AI video, choose a **Video model** and a style. **AI Style** matches the color, lighting, and camera feel of your footage. You can also pick a preset style.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/WMXFusCPuQBQuKUM/images/imgs/ai-broll-fill-with.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=WMXFusCPuQBQuKUM\&q=85\&s=a804e603463a31e5e6e90f43d7b0f646)

* Click **Add B-rolls**. The B-roll is added to your timeline.

## Add B-roll at a specific moment

### From the timeline and the B-roll panel

* In the B-roll panel, click **Add B-roll**.
* Move the playhead to where you want B-roll and click **Add at \[time]**.
* Describe your idea in **Enter prompt**, click **Upload media**, or click **Search stock footage**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/WMXFusCPuQBQuKUM/images/imgs/ai-broll-add-at-time.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=WMXFusCPuQBQuKUM\&q=85\&s=728fde8c7a6580bd29987c7c03d3756a)

### From a specific sentence

* Simply drag-and-highlight part of the transcript you would like to add B-roll to, and then select the **Add** option to trigger the **Add B-roll** prompt box.
* Describe your idea in **Enter prompt**, click **Upload media**, or click **Search stock footage**.

## Add B-roll when you create a project

You can add B-roll automatically to your video up to 120 minutes.

* Select **AI B-roll** from the dashboard.
* Add your video and choose **AI video B-roll**, **AI image B-roll**, or **Stock video B-roll**.
* Click **Create** or **Add B-roll in one click**. B-roll is added to your video automatically.

## Edit or regenerate B-roll

* **Move or trim:** drag a B-roll on the timeline, or drag its edges.
* **Regenerate:** click a B-roll, then change the prompt or style and generate again.
* **Go back to earlier results:** open **Recent B-roll** in the B-roll panel.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is B-roll available for everyone?

AI image B-roll and stock B-roll are available on **Trial**, **Starter**, and higher plans, while AI video B-roll is available for **Trial, Pro, Max, and Business** plans. The B-roll feature is currently not available for free plan users.

### Why did Find spots return nothing?

Yes. Open the B-roll panel and click **Recent B-roll** to see earlier results for this clip and add them back.

### Will Find spots replace the B-roll I already added?

It replaces Auto B-roll at the spots you select. B-roll you added manually is kept.

### Do failed generations use credits?

No. Credits for failed images or clips are returned.

### What's the difference between AI Style and a Preset style for Video B-roll?

**AI Style** is the default style for AI video B-roll. It looks at your original footage and matches its color, lighting, and camera feel, so the B-roll blends in with your video.

If you want a specific look instead, pick a preset style before you click **Add B-rolls**.

### Can I edit generated B-roll?

Currently, you have the ability to drag and reframe the B-roll.

You are also able to move B-roll on the timeline by clicking-and-holding it and moving it wherever you please on the timeline. Furthermore, you can also extend or trim B-roll.

Please note: For Stock B-roll, you are not able to extend it past the original stock footage.

### Can I regenerate B-roll?

Absolutely! Simply click on the B-roll you wish to change. You can revise the prompt, or for AI video, change the style, then generate again to get a fresh B-roll.

### How much does B-roll cost?

AI image and AI video B-roll use credits:

* **In the editor**: priced per B-roll
* **When you create a project**: priced per minute of video

You'll see the exact credit cost before you add B-roll. Credits for failed generations are returned. Stock video B-roll is free.

### Am I able to search for B-roll from the stock library?

Yes. In the B-roll panel, click **Or add B-roll manually**, then click **Search stock footage** and type in a keyword to pull the perfect B-roll clips from the stock library.

### Can I move or extend/trim B-roll?

You are also able to move B-roll on the timeline by clicking-and-holding it and moving it wherever you please on the timeline. Furthermore, you can also extend (image B-roll only) or trim B-roll as per your preferences.
