# Trim and Extend the Video

## Add a new section to your clip

If you want to find a better hook, extend your clip, or add a new section from other parts of your video to the clip, you can simply click the **Add a section** button located at the beginning or at the end of the caption area, and choose which section you'd like to add from the whole transcript.

## Trim unwanted footage

To make your clip concise and engaging, you can trim unwanted sentences using two methods:

Method 1: Select the section you want to remove from the text and click **Delete**.

Method 2: Drag the beginning and end of selected segment in the timeline editor to trim it.
