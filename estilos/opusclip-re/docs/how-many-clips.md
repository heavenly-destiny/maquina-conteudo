# How Many Clips Can I Get?

The number of clips that Opus creates is dependent on the video length that you process, and the model you use. ClipAnything usually yields slightly more clips than ClipBasic:

* 0-3 min: 1-2 clips
* 3-10 min: 3-14 clips
* 10-30 min: 5-21 clips
* 30-60 min: 23-32 clips
* 60-120 min: 32-42 clips
* Over 120 min: 42-55 clips

We are working on improving our algorithm so that we will generate more clips for you. Stay tuned!
