# Add sound effects automatically with Auto SFX

Sound design can make the difference between a flat clip and one that stops the scroll. Auto SFX watches your video, detects key moments, and places production-quality sound effects on your timeline automatically — no editing experience needed.

## How to apply auto SFX

### From the recipe page

Turn on Auto SFX in your recipe to apply it once, or save it to a custom recipe to apply it automatically every time.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/Ampo3Fel6awlejmS/images/imgs/auto-sfx-recipe-page.jpg?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=Ampo3Fel6awlejmS\&q=85\&s=35d94d57b27f10990e1daefb9191fd42)

### From the editor

1. Open a clip in the editor and click **AI enhance** in the right sidebar, then click **Auto sound effects**. You can also click **Audio** in the right sidebar and open the **Sound effects** tab.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/Ampo3Fel6awlejmS/images/imgs/auto-sfx-editor-audio-menu.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=Ampo3Fel6awlejmS\&q=85\&s=111e641f3e344265384693fb117449e8)

2. Click **Add sound effects**. The number of credits it uses is shown on the button. It takes about 30 seconds for most clips.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/Ampo3Fel6awlejmS/images/imgs/auto-sfx-add-sound-effects.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=Ampo3Fel6awlejmS\&q=85\&s=7b2ec43cd061f76e7d7b6cc9e5e63bba)

3. Your sound effects appear on the timeline and in the **Sound effects** tab. Click any sound in the list to play your clip from just before it.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/Ampo3Fel6awlejmS/images/imgs/auto-sfx-timeline.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=Ampo3Fel6awlejmS\&q=85\&s=1c356863754119d4eb5555adfeb040ca)

If your clip doesn't need any sound effects, for example a music video, you'll see **No sound effects needed for this clip**, and you won't be charged.

## Edit your sound effects

Every sound effect is a block on your timeline. You can:

* **Move** a sound by dragging it
* **Change its volume** by clicking it
* **Split** or **delete** a sound like any other block

## How many credits does Auto SFX use?

7 credits per minute of clip, rounded up to the next minute. You will not be charged if Auto SFX fails or doesn't add any sounds.

## FAQ

**How long does it take?**

About 30 seconds for most clips. Longer videos take longer.

**What kinds of sounds does it add?**

OpusClip adds sounds for transitions, impacts, UI moments, reactions and more, matched to your video's content and tone. Every sound comes from a curated library, so it sounds clean and consistent.

**Why didn't Auto SFX add a sound where I expected one?**

Auto SFX only adds a sound where it fits the moment, and skips some to keep your clip from feeling cluttered.

**Can I undo the sound effects?**

Yes. Delete any sound on the timeline without affecting the rest. To run Auto SFX again, delete all of its sounds, then click **Add sound effects**. Each run uses credits.

**Why do the sounds sound different from before?**

Auto SFX used to generate each sound with AI. It now picks sounds from a curated library, so they're cleaner and timed more precisely.

**Does Auto SFX work in any language?**

Yes. Auto SFX looks at what happens on screen as well as what's said, so it works with any language.

**Is there a length limit?**

No. Longer videos take longer to process and use more credits.

**Will this change my original video?**

No. Sound effects are added to the clip you're editing and can be removed at any time. Your original upload stays unchanged.
