# Add Music

Want to make your clips more exciting and engaging? Now you can easily add copyright free music to your clips. Choose a track from our music library or upload your own music files to set the perfect tone. Here's how to get started:

> [!NOTE]
>
> Although our music is licensed and generally free from copyright claims, sometimes claims may occur on videos due to mismatches from other companies or individuals fraudulently claiming our content. If this happens, please contact us and we will investigate and help resolve the issue. Please also ensure that you are following the NCS usage policy as outlined here: [https://ncs.io/usage-policy](https://ncs.io/usage-policy).
>
> Additionally, we recommend visiting: [https://ncs.io/](https://ncs.io/).
>
> Use the "Search tracks & artists" function to find the specific track you used. Once you open the track page, you will see the required credit section in the description. Make sure you copy and paste that exact credit format into your video description to help prevent copyright claims.

1. **Navigate to the Editor:** Open the specific clip editor page you want to work on.
2. **We provide two ways to select music files**
   1. **Bring your own Music**
      In the **Audio** panel, click **Upload** to open the Add audio dialog. There are two ways in:

      * **Upload a file** — audio (mp3, m4a, ogg, wav) or video (mp4, mov, mkv). Give us a video and we keep the sound.
      * **Paste a link** — a public video or audio URL. We fetch it and pull the audio out.

      It's also saved to **Media › Audio** and **Asset Library**, so you can reuse it in any other clip without uploading again.

      Only use audio you own or have permission to use.

      ![The Add audio dialog with Click to upload or drop a file here, and a Paste a video or audio link field.](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/qhqzUq7cOIwYVOOV/images/imgs/add-music-add-audio.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=qhqzUq7cOIwYVOOV\&q=85\&s=935be510a89c0ea1a0249f940268678c)
   2. **Select from the music library:**
      * Go to the right panel and select **"Music"** and look up the music you want to add to the list.
      * You can switch the type of music or search for your favorite music
      * If you come across a favorite song, you can also click to favorite it, so that you can quickly find it under the Liked category in the future.
      * Click the play button to preview the music first

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P/images/imgs/screenshot0409-07.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P\&q=85\&s=43171ca516dd6c352638f2c25a622b5a)

3. **Add music to tracks:** There are two ways to add music to tracks
   1. Click the **Add** button on the music file , and the file will be added to the soundtrack after the timeline
   2. Drag the music and drop it into the specified position on the soundtrack
4. **Cut the music to the appropriate length** and remove excess parts
   1. Select the music file on the soundtrack you want to edit, and position the timeline at the desired cut point.
   2. Click the split button to split the audio into two segments
   3. You can delete unnecessary segments by clicking the delete button
5. **Adjust the music:** Select the music clip on the soundtrack and use the properties panel on the right.
   * **Volume:** Drag the slider to set how loud the music sits under your video (15% by default).
   * **Speed:** Play the track faster or slower (from 0.5× to 2.5×).
   * **Fade in / Fade out:** Set up to 5 seconds on each side so the music eases in and out instead of cutting. This also works on sound-effect tracks (0 seconds by default).
   * If your track contains multiple pieces of music, simply select **"Apply settings to all"** to apply the same settings across all tracks.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/POE01J53Yu5PFrlr/images/imgs/add-music-properties-panel.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=POE01J53Yu5PFrlr\&q=85\&s=c4824e38244d89fdd8a7eab99867c743)

6. **Delete the music files on the track**
   1. Select the segment you want to delete and click the Delete button.

## FAQs and More

Is there a maximum upload limit for audio files?

* Yes, at the moment, the platform supports up to a maximum of 30 minutes per file.

Is music copyrighted? Will there be copyright issues when using it?

* Yes, our music library contains copyright-free and licensed music that you can add to your clips. However, sometimes claims or copyright issues may occur on videos due to mismatches from other companies or individuals fraudulently claiming our content. If this happens, please [contact us](https://www.opus.pro/contact-support) and we will investigate and help resolve the issue.
