# Things You Should Set Up First

Welcome to OpusClip! Below you will find the top things to set up to get you started with OpusClip.

## Create Your Brand Kit

**Brand Kit** is a place where you can set up your branding preferences and apply them to all of your clips. To set up your Brand Kit, click **Brand kit** in the left sidebar or visit [here](https://clip.opus.pro/brand-templates).

On the **Brand kit** page, you can set up your preferences. Starter users can set up 1 Brand Kit, and Pro users can set up up to 4 Brand Kits. Free users can apply the default Brand Kit in the Editor, but need to upgrade to create one.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/5TopqQ1f1asfSLn4/images/imgs/snapshot-112.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=5TopqQ1f1asfSLn4\&q=85\&s=41b46ce325607568f26e651fdc4185d0)

On the left hand side are the settings that will change your **Brand Kit**. You can click onto the areas to expand out the settings.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P/images/imgs/screenshot0408-01.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P\&q=85\&s=d2952e9acff26a454454ce5b2fa26ae4)

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P/images/imgs/screenshot0408-02.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P\&q=85\&s=3a392269aa3d28a468c376fac916446a)

Click **Save template** at the top right hand corner or from template name dropdown as shown in the screenshot below.

## Add Your Social Accounts for 1-Click Posting

You can connect your social media accounts to make it easier to post directly from OpusClip. Go to the left tool bar and click **Social Accounts**. When hovering over it, it will display Social Accounts.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC/images/imgs/snapshot-116.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC\&q=85\&s=b2be4a08ce74a7a8a88e907a65e9af9b)

Once you've done so, a popup menu will open showing your currently connected accounts and it will also allow you to add new accounts by clicking on the **Add Account button**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC/images/imgs/snapshot-117.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC\&q=85\&s=f7f718d0c7ab7db7cb9bc0ecb3f16d8c)

Click the **Add Account button**, select your desired social media platform, and follow the steps in the pop-up to complete the connection.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC/images/imgs/snapshot-118.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=wWf8oL-5UZ9lZUDC\&q=85\&s=c859a2db860eaefd214bc12e26151a45)

## Setup Your Team Workplace

You can create a **Team Workplace** and invite your teammates to join. Here is how you can do this:

1. Click your **Profile image** on the left sidebar and go to **Create a team**

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P/images/imgs/screenshot0408-03.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=7i57AL9_SzMSJI7P\&q=85\&s=a0e349137d80d3b824150e4d423ddbe8)

2. Name your **Team Workspace**, add a logo and create the team

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/CsfV__T0-LAmxfr6/images/imgs/add-team-2.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=CsfV__T0-LAmxfr6\&q=85\&s=42da578d10670081b00aac0061dd26e0)

3. Copy the **invitation link** and send it to your teammates.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/CsfV__T0-LAmxfr6/images/imgs/add-team-3.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=CsfV__T0-LAmxfr6\&q=85\&s=100376947ea20c4750a1e75fbb84baed)

4. Then your teammates can join the team by clicking into the **invitation link**.

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/CsfV__T0-LAmxfr6/images/imgs/add-team-4.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=CsfV__T0-LAmxfr6\&q=85\&s=d26f0fce080267a263e35eda2d649249)

5. As the **team owner**, you can also manage the team members on the **Team settings** page

![](https://mintcdn.com/opusclip-c3e48c12/CsfV__T0-LAmxfr6/images/imgs/add-team-5.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=CsfV__T0-LAmxfr6\&q=85\&s=a99ce15d4414b8205f65931872dd84ec)
